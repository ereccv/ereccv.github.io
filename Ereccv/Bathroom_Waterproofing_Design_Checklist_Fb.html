    What this is built on

    • NCC 2022 clause text: ABCB Housing Provisions Part 10.2 and Volume One Part F2 with Specification 26.
    • AS 3740:2021 Waterproofing of domestic wet areas, as called up by both NCC 2022 and NCC 2025.
    • WA Building and Energy Industry Bulletin 177 (August 2026) and General Inspection Report 10 on shower waterproofing.
    • WA adopted NCC 2025 on 1 May 2026 (WA IB 176). WA permit authorities advise that NCC 2022 can still be used for permit applications until 30 April 2027.
    • ABCB guidance that the 1500 mm for an unenclosed shower is measured from the shower rose itself, not from the wall.

    Check before it goes on a drawing

    • AS 3740:2021 is a paid standard and was not read directly. Its clause numbers and requirements here come from the WA bulletin, the published contents list and industry guides. Confirm them in a licensed copy.
    • The NCC 2025 clause text for Part 10.2 could not be opened. Published summaries report two small changes: fibre-cement sheet wording in 10.2.9, and 10.2.14 now spells out the options for enclosed and unenclosed showers.
    • Wall membrane height is given from the finished shower floor (HP 10.2.25). HP 10.2.2 and S26C3 measure 1800 mm from the floor substrate, so this is the safer reading.

    Not covered

    • Baths, shower-over-bath, preformed shower bases, laundries, balconies and external areas.
    • Accessible showers to AS 1428.1.
    • Livable housing design provisions, which remain voluntary in WA.
    • Performance Solutions. Everything here is the Deemed-to-Satisfy route.
    • Membrane manufacturer rules, which sit on top of the code: primer, film thickness, cure time and compatibility.

    This is a design aid, not a certification. The approved drawings, the building surveyor's Certificate of Design Compliance and the membrane manufacturer's instructions govern the job.
