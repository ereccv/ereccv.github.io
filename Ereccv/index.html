About

I am a detail-oriented AutoCAD drafter. My work is turning architectural and engineering plans into clear drawings that contractors can build from, and keeping drawing packages accurate so projects stay on schedule.

I started in telecommunications design, spent six years in the CAD team at a broadcast company, and now work on tile layout and interior fit-out drafting for an Australian client. I also prepare cost estimates in CostX and support procurement and HSE documentation.
